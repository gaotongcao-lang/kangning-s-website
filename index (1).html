01 / About

Understanding people in context.

Kangning Du is a lecturer in the School of Psychological Sciences at Macquarie University.

Her work brings together social and developmental psychology, cross-cultural perspectives and mental wellbeing. She is interested in how people relate to one another across different ages, identities and communities.

02 / Research

Questions that connect us.

Kangning’s research interests span human connection, social attitudes and equitable mental health support.

  • Empathy and prosocial behaviour across the lifespan
  • Social understanding and child development
  • Prejudice, intergroup bias and prejudice reduction
  • Social identity and attitudes toward outgroups
  • Cross-cultural psychology
  • Asian mental health and wellbeing
  • Sustainable mental wellbeing
  • Mental health in rural and underserved communities
  • AI and psychological or mental health support
03 / Psychological support

Support shaped around the person.

Kangning’s areas of psychological service include individual therapy and counselling, with attention to life context, culture and wellbeing.

  • Individual psychological therapy and counselling
  • Mental health and wellbeing support
  • Stress, adjustment and life transitions
  • Cross-cultural and migrant mental health
  • Support for young people and adults
  • Bilingual services in English and Mandarin Chinese
  • Experience supporting ADHD and autism assessments
