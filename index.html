About

Most of my work comes back to one question: how people come to understand each other.

Across ages, across cultures, and across the distance between needing support and finding it. I study it as a researcher, meet it as a psychologist, and teach it at Macquarie University, where I am a Lecturer in the School of Psychological Sciences.

I did my PhD at the University of Otago in New Zealand, and I am registered as a psychologist in both New Zealand and Australia. Working between two countries, and between English and Chinese, has made culture feel less like a variable and more like the place people actually live.

Away from work: photographs taken on long walks, and a lot of Lana Del Rey.
