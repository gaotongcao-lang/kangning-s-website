01 / About

Kangning Du is a Lecturer in the School of Psychological Sciences at Macquarie University and a Registered Psychologist in Australia and New Zealand.

Her work sits at the intersection of psychology research, education, and practice. Her interests span social and developmental psychology, cross-cultural perspectives, and mental wellbeing, with a focus on understanding how people connect, adapt, and thrive across different stages of life and cultural contexts.
