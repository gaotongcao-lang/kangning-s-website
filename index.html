01 / About

Psychology in context

Kangning Du is a lecturer in the School of Psychological Sciences at Macquarie University.

Her interests connect social and developmental psychology, cross-cultural perspectives, and mental wellbeing. She explores how people understand one another and how psychological knowledge can better support diverse communities.
